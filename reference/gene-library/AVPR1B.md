# AVPR1B: arginine vasopressin receptor 1B

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 1 (neuroendocrine and peptide signaling). The protein encoded by this gene acts as receptor for arginine vasopressin. This receptor belongs to the subfamily of G-protein coupled receptors which includes AVPR1A, V2R and OXT receptors.

**Corpus relevance:** Peptide-hormone and related signaling modulates sexual function, stress, and behavior, symptom domains prominent in PSSD/PFS.

**Protein (UniProt):** Vasopressin V1b receptor ([P47901](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P47901/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Receptor for arginine vasopressin. The activity of this receptor is mediated by G proteins which activate a phosphatidyl-inositol-calcium second messenger system.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.
