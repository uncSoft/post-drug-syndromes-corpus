# TAF2: TATA-box binding protein associated factor 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (basal transcription machinery). Initiation of transcription by RNA polymerase II requires the activities of more than 70 polypeptides. The protein that coordinates these activities is transcription factor IID (TFIID), which binds to the core promoter to position the polymerase properly, serves as the scaffold for assembly of the remainder of the transcription complex, and acts as a channel for regulatory signals.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Transcription initiation factor TFIID subunit 2 ([Q6P1X5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q6P1X5/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** The TFIID basal transcription factor complex plays a major role in the initiation of RNA polymerase II (Pol II)-dependent transcription. TFIID recognizes and binds promoters with or without a TATA box via its subunit TBP, a TATA-box-binding protein, and promotes assembly of the pre-initiation complex (PIC). The TFIID complex consists of TBP and TBP-associated factors (TAFs), including TAF1, TAF2, TAF3, TAF4, TAF5, TAF6, TAF7, TAF8, TAF9, TAF10, TAF11, TAF12 and TAF13. TAF2 forms a promoter DNA binding subcomplex of TFIID, together with TAF7 and TAF1.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in all tissues tested.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Neurodevelopmental disorder with feeding difficulties, thin corpus callosum, and foot deformity (NEDFCF).
