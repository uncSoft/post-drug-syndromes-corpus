# GATA2: GATA binding protein 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Encodes a member of the GATA family of zinc-finger transcription factors that are named for the consensus nucleotide sequence they bind in the promoter regions of target genes. The protein plays an essential role in regulating transcription of genes involved in the development and proliferation of hematopoietic and endocrine cell lineages.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Endothelial transcription factor GATA-2 ([P23769](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P23769/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transcriptional activator which regulates endothelin-1 gene expression in endothelial cells. Binds to the consensus sequence 5'-AGATAG-3'.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Endothelial cells.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Immunodeficiency 21 (IMD21); Lymphedema, primary, with myelodysplasia (LMPM); Myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS).
