# AHCY: adenosylhomocysteinase

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 20 (methylation and one-carbon metabolism). S-adenosylhomocysteine hydrolase belongs to the adenosylhomocysteinase family. It catalyzes the reversible hydrolysis of S-adenosylhomocysteine (AdoHcy) to adenosine (Ado) and L-homocysteine (Hcy).

**Corpus relevance:** One-carbon metabolism supplies methyl groups for DNA/histone methylation, the epigenetic layer implicated in persistent post-drug changes.

**Protein (UniProt):** Adenosylhomocysteinase ([P23526](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P23526/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Catalyzes the hydrolysis of S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine to form adenosine and homocysteine. Binds copper ions (By similarity).

**Pathway (UniProt):** Amino-acid biosynthesis; L-homocysteine biosynthesis; L-homocysteine from S-adenosyl-L-homocysteine: step 1/1.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Melanosome; Nucleus; Endoplasmic reticulum.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypermethioninemia with S-adenosylhomocysteine hydrolase deficiency (HMAHCHD).
