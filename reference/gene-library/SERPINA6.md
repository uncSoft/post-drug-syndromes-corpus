# SERPINA6: serpin family A member 6

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 14 (hpg and gonadal axis). Encodes an alpha-globulin protein with corticosteroid-binding properties. This is the major transport protein for glucorticoids and progestins in the blood of most vertebrates.

**Corpus relevance:** HPG-axis signaling and steroid carrier proteins govern testosterone production and bioavailability. Whether this axis is disturbed in post-drug syndromes is unsettled: the library holds no LH or FSH measurement in men with persistent symptoms after finasteride or an SSRI, average testosterone and DHT were normal in the controlled study (SIDE-010), and 13% of 24 men in a smaller series had confirmed low levels of both (SIDE-008).

**Protein (UniProt):** Corticosteroid-binding globulin ([P08185](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P08185/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Major transport protein for glucocorticoids and progestins in the blood of almost all vertebrate species.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Secreted.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Plasma; synthesized in liver. Has also been identified in a number of glycocorticoid responsive cells.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Corticosteroid-binding globulin deficiency (CBG deficiency).
