# CHD7: chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 7

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (epigenetic regulation). Encodes a protein that contains several helicase family domains. Mutations in this gene have been found in some patients with the CHARGE syndrome.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler CHD7 ([Q9P2D1](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9P2D1/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin-remodeling factor, slides nucleosomes along DNA; nucleosome sliding requires ATP. Probable transcription regulator. May be involved in the in 45S precursor rRNA production.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Nucleus, nucleolus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Widely expressed in fetal and adult tissues.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** CHARGE syndrome (CHARGES); Scoliosis, isolated, 3 (IS3); Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism 5 with or without anosmia (HH5).
