# RXRB: retinoid X receptor beta

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 6 (steroid and nuclear hormone receptors). Encodes a member of the retinoid X receptor (RXR) family of nuclear receptors which are involved in mediating the effects of retinoic acid (RA). The protein forms homodimers with the retinoic acid, thyroid hormone, and vitamin D receptors, increasing both DNA binding and transcriptional function on their respective response elements.

**Corpus relevance:** Nuclear receptors transduce steroid signals into lasting gene-expression programs; persistent receptor-level remodeling (or silencing) is a leading hypothesis for symptoms that outlast drug exposure.

**Protein (UniProt):** Retinoic acid receptor RXR-beta ([P28702](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P28702/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Receptor for retinoic acid. Retinoic acid receptors bind as heterodimers to their target response elements in response to their ligands, all-trans or 9-cis retinoic acid, and regulate gene expression in various biological processes. The RAR/RXR heterodimers bind to the retinoic acid response elements (RARE).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Cytoplasm.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in aortic endothelial cells (at protein level). Expressed in monocytes. Expressed in a variety of tumor cell lines.
