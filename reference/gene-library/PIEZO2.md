# PIEZO2: piezo type mechanosensitive ion channel component 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 18 (sensory and ion channels). The protein encoded by this gene contains more than thirty transmembrane domains and likely functions as part of mechanically-activated (MA) cation channels. These channels serve to connect mechanical forces to biological signals.

**Corpus relevance:** Gap-junction and ion-channel genes; no established post-drug-syndrome link; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Piezo-type mechanosensitive ion channel component 2 ([Q9H5I5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9H5I5/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Pore-forming subunit of the mechanosensitive non-specific cation Piezo channel required for rapidly adapting mechanically activated (MA) currents and has a key role in sensing touch and tactile pain. Piezo channels are homotrimeric three-blade propeller-shaped structures that utilize a cap-motion and plug-and-latch mechanism to gate their ion-conducting pathways. Expressed in sensory neurons, is essential for diverse physiological processes, including respiratory control, systemic metabolism, urinary function, and proprioception (By similarity). Mediates airway stretch sensing, enabling efficient respiration at birth and maintaining normal breathing in adults (By similarity). It regulates brown and beige adipose tissue morphology and function, preventing systemic hypermetabolism (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Arthrogryposis, distal, 5 (DA5); Arthrogryposis, distal, 3 (DA3); Marden-Walker syndrome (MWKS); Arthrogryposis, distal, with impaired proprioception and touch (DAIPT).
