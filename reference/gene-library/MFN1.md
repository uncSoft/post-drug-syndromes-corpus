# MFN1: mitofusin 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (mitochondrial function and energy). The protein encoded by this gene is a mediator of mitochondrial fusion. This protein and mitofusin 2 are homologs of the Drosophila protein fuzzy onion (Fzo).

**Corpus relevance:** No record in the library measures mitochondrial function or oxidative stress in people with these syndromes; the records that touch either are animal or cell work, and they point in mixed directions. These genes are here for their place in energy metabolism, not for an established link.

**Protein (UniProt):** Mitofusin-1 ([Q8IWA4](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8IWA4/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Mitochondrial outer membrane GTPase that mediates mitochondrial clustering and fusion. Membrane clustering requires GTPase activity. It may involve a major rearrangement of the coiled coil domains. Mitochondria are highly dynamic organelles, and their morphology is determined by the equilibrium between mitochondrial fusion and fission events. Overexpression induces the formation of mitochondrial networks (in vitro). Has low GTPase activity.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion outer membrane; Cytoplasm.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Detected in kidney and heart (at protein level). Ubiquitous. Expressed at slightly higher level in kidney and heart. Isoform 2 may be overexpressed in some tumors, such as lung cancers.
