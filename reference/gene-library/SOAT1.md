# SOAT1: sterol O-acyltransferase 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 1 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). The protein encoded by this gene belongs to the acyltransferase family. It is located in the endoplasmic reticulum, and catalyzes the formation of fatty acid-cholesterol esters.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Protein (UniProt):** Sterol O-acyltransferase 1 ([P35610](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P35610/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Catalyzes the formation of fatty acid-cholesterol esters, which are less soluble in membranes than cholesterol. Plays a role in lipoprotein assembly and dietary cholesterol absorption. Preferentially utilizes oleoyl-CoA ((9Z)-octadecenoyl-CoA) as a substrate: shows a higher activity towards an acyl-CoA substrate with a double bond at the delta-9 position (9Z) than towards saturated acyl-CoA or an unsaturated acyl-CoA with a double bond at the delta-7 (7Z) or delta-11 (11Z) positions.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Endoplasmic reticulum membrane.
