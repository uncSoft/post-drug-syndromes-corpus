# POLR2I: RNA polymerase II subunit I

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 19 (basal transcription machinery). Encodes a subunit of RNA polymerase II, the polymerase responsible for synthesizing messenger RNA in eukaryotes. This subunit, in combination with two other polymerase subunits, forms the DNA binding domain of the polymerase, a groove in which the DNA template is transcribed into RNA.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** DNA-directed RNA polymerase II subunit RPB9 ([P36954](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P36954/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Core component of RNA polymerase II (Pol II), a DNA-dependent RNA polymerase which synthesizes mRNA precursors and many functional non-coding RNAs using the four ribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates. Pol II is the central component of the basal RNA polymerase II transcription machinery. It is composed of mobile elements that move relative to each other. POLR2I/RPB9 is part of the upper jaw surrounding the central large cleft and thought to grab the incoming DNA template.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus, nucleolus.
