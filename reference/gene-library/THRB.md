# THRB: thyroid hormone receptor beta

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (steroid and nuclear hormone receptors). The protein encoded by this gene is a nuclear hormone receptor for triiodothyronine. It is one of the several receptors for thyroid hormone, and has been shown to mediate the biological activities of thyroid hormone.

**Corpus relevance:** Nuclear receptors transduce steroid signals into lasting gene-expression programs; persistent receptor-level remodeling (or silencing) is a leading hypothesis for symptoms that outlast drug exposure.

**Protein (UniProt):** Thyroid hormone receptor beta ([P10828](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P10828/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Nuclear hormone receptor that can act as a repressor or activator of transcription. High affinity receptor for thyroid hormones, including triiodothyronine and thyroxine.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Thyroid hormone resistance, generalized, autosomal dominant (GRTHD); Thyroid hormone resistance, generalized, autosomal recessive (GRTHR); Selective pituitary thyroid hormone resistance (PRTH).
