# AKR1B10: aldo-keto reductase family 1 member B10

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 7 (drug metabolism (phase i)). Encodes a member of the aldo/keto reductase superfamily, which consists of more than 40 known enzymes and proteins. This member can efficiently reduce aliphatic and aromatic aldehydes, and it is less active on hexoses.

**Corpus relevance:** Phase I enzymes determine exposure to finasteride, SSRIs, and other implicated drugs; variation here shapes the likelihood and persistence of adverse effects.

**Protein (UniProt):** Aldo-keto reductase family 1 member B10 ([O60218](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O60218/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Catalyzes the NADPH-dependent reduction of a wide variety of carbonyl-containing compounds to their corresponding alcohols. Displays strong enzymatic activity toward all-trans-retinal, 9-cis-retinal, and 13-cis-retinal. Plays a critical role in detoxifying dietary and lipid-derived unsaturated carbonyls, such as crotonaldehyde, 4-hydroxynonenal, trans-2-hexenal, trans-2,4-hexadienal and their glutathione-conjugates carbonyls (GS-carbonyls). Displays no reductase activity towards glucose.

**Pathway (UniProt):** Cofactor metabolism; retinol metabolism.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Lysosome; Secreted.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Found in many tissues. Highly expressed in small intestine, colon and adrenal gland.
