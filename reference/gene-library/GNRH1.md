# GNRH1: gonadotropin releasing hormone 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (hpg and gonadal axis). Encodes a preproprotein that is proteolytically processed to generate a peptide that is a member of the gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) family of peptides. Alternative splicing results in multiple transcript variants, at least one of which is secreted and then cleaved to generate gonadoliberin-1 and GnRH-associated peptide 1.

**Corpus relevance:** HPG-axis signaling and steroid carrier proteins govern testosterone production and bioavailability. Whether this axis is disturbed in post-drug syndromes is unsettled: the library holds no LH or FSH measurement in men with persistent symptoms after finasteride or an SSRI, average testosterone and DHT were normal in the controlled study (SIDE-010), and 13% of 24 men in a smaller series had confirmed low levels of both (SIDE-008).

**Protein (UniProt):** Progonadoliberin-1 ([P01148](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P01148/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Stimulates the secretion of gonadotropins; it stimulates the secretion of both luteinizing and follicle-stimulating hormones.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Secreted.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism 12 with or without anosmia (HH12).
