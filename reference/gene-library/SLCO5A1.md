# SLCO5A1: solute carrier organic anion transporter family member 5A1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Predicted to enable transmembrane transporter activity. Predicted to be involved in sodium-independent organic anion transport.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.
