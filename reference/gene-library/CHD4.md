# CHD4: chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 4

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 12 (epigenetic regulation). The product of this gene belongs to the SNF2/RAD54 helicase family. It represents the main component of the nucleosome remodeling and deacetylase complex and plays an important role in epigenetic transcriptional repression.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler CHD4 ([Q14839](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q14839/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin-remodeling factor that binds and distorts nucleosomal DNA. Acts as a component of the histone deacetylase NuRD complex which participates in the remodeling of chromatin. Localizes to acetylated damaged chromatin in a ZMYND8-dependent manner, to promote transcriptional repression and double-strand break repair by homologous recombination. Involved in neurogenesis (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton, microtubule organizing center, centrosome.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Widely expressed.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Sifrim-Hitz-Weiss syndrome (SIHIWES).
