# KDM4C: lysine demethylase 4C

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 9 (epigenetic regulation). This gene is a member of the Jumonji domain 2 (JMJD2) family. The protein is a trimethylation-specific demethylase, and converts specific trimethylated histone residues to the dimethylated form.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Lysine-specific demethylase 4C ([Q9H3R0](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9H3R0/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Histone demethylase that specifically demethylates 'Lys-9' and 'Lys-36' residues of histone H3, thereby playing a central role in histone code. Does not demethylate histone H3 'Lys-4', H3 'Lys-27' nor H4 'Lys-20'. Demethylates trimethylated H3 'Lys-9' and H3 'Lys-36' residue, while it has no activity on mono- and dimethylated residues. Demethylation of Lys residue generates formaldehyde and succinate.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Overexpressed in several esophageal squamous cell carcinomas (ESCs).
