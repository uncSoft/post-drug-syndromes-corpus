# MXI1: MAX interactor 1, dimerization protein

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 10 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Expression of the c-myc gene, which produces an oncogenic transcription factor, is tightly regulated in normal cells but is frequently deregulated in human cancers. The protein encoded by this gene is a transcriptional repressor thought to negatively regulate MYC function, and is therefore a potential tumor suppressor.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Max-interacting protein 1 ([P50539](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P50539/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transcriptional repressor. MXI1 binds with MAX to form a sequence-specific DNA-binding protein complex which recognizes the core sequence 5'-CAC[GA]TG-3'. MXI1 thus antagonizes MYC transcriptional activity by competing for MAX.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** High levels found in the brain, heart and lung while lower levels are seen in the liver, kidney and skeletal muscle.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Prostate cancer (PC).
