# ABCC8: ATP binding cassette subfamily C member 8

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The protein encoded by this gene is a member of the superfamily of ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters. ABC proteins transport various molecules across extra- and intra-cellular membranes.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Powers note (his unpublished theorizing, research only, not medical advice):** Powers named the ABCC transporter family (2026 summit interview, unpublished) as intracellular transporter genes implicated in metabolite clearance in his experimentally verifiable mechanism; individual ABCC members not separately named.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-binding cassette sub-family C member 8 ([Q09428](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q09428/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Regulator subunit of pancreatic ATP-sensitive potassium channel (KATP), playing a major role in the regulation of insulin release. In pancreatic cells, it forms KATP channels with KCNJ11; KCNJ11 forms the channel pore while ABCC8 is required for activation and regulation.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Leucine-induced hypoglycemia (LIH); Hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia, familial, 1 (HHF1); Diabetes mellitus, permanent neonatal, 3 (PNDM3); Transient neonatal diabetes mellitus 2 (TNDM2); Maturity-onset diabetes of the young 12 (MODY12).
