# MT-ND1: NADH dehydrogenase subunit 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome MT (mitochondrial) (mitochondrial function and energy). Enables NADH dehydrogenase (ubiquinone) activity. Involved in mitochondrial electron transport, NADH to ubiquinone and mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I assembly.

**Corpus relevance:** No record in the library measures mitochondrial function or oxidative stress in people with these syndromes; the records that touch either are animal or cell work, and they point in mixed directions. These genes are here for their place in energy metabolism, not for an established link.

**Protein (UniProt):** NADH-ubiquinone oxidoreductase chain 1 ([P03886](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P03886/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Core subunit of the mitochondrial membrane respiratory chain NADH dehydrogenase (Complex I) which catalyzes electron transfer from NADH through the respiratory chain, using ubiquinone as an electron acceptor. Essential for the catalytic activity and assembly of complex I.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion inner membrane.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Leber hereditary optic neuropathy (LHON); Mitochondrial encephalomyopathy with lactic acidosis and stroke-like episodes syndrome (MELAS); Alzheimer disease mitochondrial (AD-MT); Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2D).
