# CHD2: chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 15 (epigenetic regulation). Belongs to the CHD family, proteins characterized by chromo (chromatin organization modifier) domains and SNF2-related helicase/ATPase domains. CHD genes alter gene expression possibly by modification of chromatin structure, altering access of the transcriptional apparatus to its chromosomal DNA template.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler CHD2 ([O14647](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O14647/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin-remodeling factor that specifically binds to the promoter of target genes, leading to chromatin remodeling, possibly by promoting deposition of histone H3.3. Involved in myogenesis via interaction with MYOD1: binds to myogenic gene regulatory sequences and mediates incorporation of histone H3.3 prior to the onset of myogenic gene expression, promoting their expression (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy 94 (DEE94).
