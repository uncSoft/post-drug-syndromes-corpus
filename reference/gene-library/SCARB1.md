# SCARB1: scavenger receptor class B member 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 12 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). The protein encoded by this gene is a plasma membrane receptor for high density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL). The protein mediates cholesterol transfer to and from HDL.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Protein (UniProt):** Scavenger receptor class B member 1 ([Q8WTV0](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8WTV0/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Receptor for different ligands such as phospholipids, cholesterol ester, lipoproteins, phosphatidylserine and apoptotic cells. Receptor for HDL, mediating selective uptake of cholesteryl ether and HDL-dependent cholesterol efflux. Also facilitates the flux of free and esterified cholesterol between the cell surface and apoB-containing lipoproteins and modified lipoproteins, although less efficiently than HDL. May be involved in the phagocytosis of apoptotic cells, via its phosphatidylserine binding activity.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane; Membrane, caveola.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Widely expressed.
