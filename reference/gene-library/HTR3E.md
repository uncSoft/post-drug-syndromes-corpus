# HTR3E: 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor 3E

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (neurotransmission). This locus encodes a 5-hydroxytryptamine (serotonin) receptor subunit. The encoded protein, subunit E, may play a role in neurotransmission in myenteric neurons.

**Corpus relevance:** Monoamine, GABA, glutamate, and cholinergic systems are the direct pharmacologic targets of SSRIs/SNRIs (PSSD) and the downstream effectors of neurosteroid signaling (PFS).

**Protein (UniProt):** 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor 3E ([A5X5Y0](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/A5X5Y0/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Forms serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine/5-HT3)-activated cation-selective channel complexes, which when activated cause fast, depolarizing responses in neurons.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Postsynaptic cell membrane; Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in adult colon and intestine.
