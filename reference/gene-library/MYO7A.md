# MYO7A: myosin VIIA

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (sensory and ion channels). This gene is a member of the myosin gene family. Myosins are mechanochemical proteins characterized by the presence of a motor domain, an actin-binding domain, a neck domain that interacts with other proteins, and a tail domain that serves as an anchor.

**Corpus relevance:** Gap-junction and ion-channel genes; no established post-drug-syndrome link; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Unconventional myosin-VIIa ([Q13402](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q13402/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Myosins are actin-based motor molecules with ATPase activity. Unconventional myosins serve in intracellular movements. Their highly divergent tails bind to membranous compartments, which are then moved relative to actin filaments. In the retina, plays an important role in the renewal of the outer photoreceptor disks. Plays an important role in the distribution and migration of retinal pigment epithelial (RPE) melanosomes and phagosomes, and in the regulation of opsin transport in retinal photoreceptors. In the inner ear, plays an important role in differentiation, morphogenesis and organization of cochlear hair cell bundles. Involved in hair-cell vesicle trafficking of aminoglycosides, which are known to induce ototoxicity (By similarity). Motor protein that is a part of the functional network formed by USH1C, USH1G, CDH23 and MYO7A that mediates mechanotransduction in cochlear hair cells. Required for normal hearing.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Cytoplasm, cell cortex; Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton; Synapse.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in the pigment epithelium and the photoreceptor cells of the retina. Also found in kidney, liver, testis, cochlea, lymphocytes. Not expressed in brain.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Usher syndrome 1B (USH1B); Deafness, autosomal recessive, 2 (DFNB2); Deafness, autosomal dominant, 11 (DFNA11).
