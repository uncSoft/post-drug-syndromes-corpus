# ABCG4: ATP binding cassette subfamily G member 4

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The protein encoded by this gene is a member of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporter superfamily. ABC proteins transport various molecules across extra- and intra-cellular membranes.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-binding cassette sub-family G member 4 ([Q9H172](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9H172/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** ATP-dependent transporter of the ATP-binding cassette (ABC) family that may be involved in the cellular efflux of sterols, in particular cholesterol and desmosterol (a cholesterol precursor), to high-density lipoprotein (HDL). May play an important role in the removal of amyloid-beta peptides from brain, in a process that can be antagonized by desmosterol. However it is unclear whether ABCG4 can directly transport amyloid-beta peptides or whether peptide export may be facilitated due to changes in the membrane lipid environment (By similarity). Induces apoptosis in various cells.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane; Cytoplasmic vesicle membrane; Endosome membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed specifically in the brain and the eye.
