# APOB: apolipoprotein B

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 2 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). This gene product is the main apolipoprotein of chylomicrons and low density lipoproteins (LDL), and is the ligand for the LDL receptor. It occurs in plasma as two main isoforms, apoB-48 and apoB-100: the former is synthesized exclusively in the gut and the latter in the liver.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Protein (UniProt):** Apolipoprotein B-100 ([P04114](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04114/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Apolipoprotein B is a major protein constituent of chylomicrons (apo B-48), LDL (apo B-100) and VLDL (apo B-100). Apo B-100 functions as a recognition signal for the cellular binding and internalization of LDL particles by the apoB/E receptor.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Secreted; Lipid droplet.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypobetalipoproteinemia, familial, 1 (FHBL1); Hypercholesterolemia, familial, 2 (FHCL2).
