# POLR2J: RNA polymerase II subunit J

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 7 (basal transcription machinery). Encodes a subunit of RNA polymerase II, the polymerase responsible for synthesizing messenger RNA in eukaryotes. The product of this gene exists as a heterodimer with another polymerase subunit; together they form a core subassembly unit of the polymerase.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** DNA-directed RNA polymerase II subunit RPB11-a ([P52435](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P52435/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Core component of RNA polymerase II (Pol II), a DNA-dependent RNA polymerase which synthesizes mRNA precursors and many functional non-coding RNAs using the four ribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitously expressed. High expression was found in heart and skeletal muscle.
