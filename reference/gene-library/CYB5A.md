# CYB5A: cytochrome b5 type A

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 18 (steroid hormone synthesis). The protein encoded by this gene is a membrane-bound cytochrome that reduces ferric hemoglobin (methemoglobin) to ferrous hemoglobin, which is required for stearyl-CoA-desaturase activity. Defects in this gene are a cause of type IV hereditary methemoglobinemia. Three transcript variants encoding different isoforms have been found for this gene.

**Corpus relevance:** Steroid-synthesis enzymes sit directly on the pathways perturbed by 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors and SSRIs; inherited variation here is a candidate susceptibility factor for persistent post-drug phenotypes.

**Protein (UniProt):** Cytochrome b5 ([P00167](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P00167/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Cytochrome b5 is a membrane-bound hemoprotein functioning as an electron carrier for several membrane-bound oxygenases.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Endoplasmic reticulum membrane; Microsome membrane; Cytoplasm.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Methemoglobinemia and ambiguous genitalia (METAG).
