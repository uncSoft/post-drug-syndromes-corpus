# HSPD1: heat shock protein family D (Hsp60) member 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 2 (steroid receptor chaperones). Encodes a member of the chaperonin family. The encoded mitochondrial protein may function as a signaling molecule in the innate immune system.

**Corpus relevance:** Chaperone/co-chaperone control of steroid-receptor folding and sensitivity is a regulatory layer above the receptors themselves, tied to stress-axis signaling.

**Protein (UniProt):** 60 kDa heat shock protein, mitochondrial ([P10809](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P10809/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Chaperonin implicated in mitochondrial protein import and macromolecular assembly. Together with Hsp10, facilitates the correct folding of imported proteins. May also prevent misfolding and promote the refolding and proper assembly of unfolded polypeptides generated under stress conditions in the mitochondrial matrix. The functional units of these chaperonins consist of heptameric rings of the large subunit Hsp60, which function as a back-to-back double ring. In a cyclic reaction, Hsp60 ring complexes bind one unfolded substrate protein per ring, followed by the binding of ATP and association with 2 heptameric rings of the co-chaperonin Hsp10. This leads to sequestration of the substrate protein in the inner cavity of Hsp60 where, for a certain period of time, it can fold undisturbed by other cell components. Synchronous hydrolysis of ATP in all Hsp60 subunits results in the dissociation of the chaperonin rings and the release of ADP and the folded substrate protein (Probable).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion matrix.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Spastic paraplegia 13, autosomal dominant (SPG13); Leukodystrophy, hypomyelinating, 4 (HLD4).
