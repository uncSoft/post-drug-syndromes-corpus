# ABCB4: ATP binding cassette subfamily B member 4

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 7 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The membrane-associated protein encoded by this gene is a member of the superfamily of ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters. ABC proteins transport various molecules across extra- and intra-cellular membranes.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Phosphatidylcholine translocator ABCB4 ([P21439](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P21439/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Isoform 1: Energy-dependent phospholipid efflux translocator that acts as a positive regulator of biliary lipid secretion. Functions as a floppase that translocates specifically phosphatidylcholine (PC) from the inner to the outer leaflet of the canalicular membrane bilayer into the canaliculi of hepatocytes. Translocation of PC makes the biliary phospholipids available for extraction into the canaliculi lumen by bile salt mixed micelles and therefore protects the biliary tree from the detergent activity of bile salts. Plays a role in the recruitment of phosphatidylcholine (PC), phosphatidylethanolamine (PE) and sphingomyelin (SM) molecules to nonraft membranes and to further enrichment of SM and cholesterol in raft membranes in hepatocytes. Required for proper phospholipid bile formation (By similarity). Indirectly involved in cholesterol efflux activity from hepatocytes into the canalicular lumen in the presence of bile salts in an ATP-dependent manner.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane; Apical cell membrane; Membrane raft; Cytoplasm; Cytoplasmic vesicle, clathrin-coated vesicle.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Cholestasis, progressive familial intrahepatic, 3 (PFIC3); Cholestasis of pregnancy, intrahepatic 3 (ICP3); Gallbladder disease 1 (GBD1).
