# DGAT2: diacylglycerol O-acyltransferase 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). Encodes one of two enzymes which catalyzes the final reaction in the synthesis of triglycerides in which diacylglycerol is covalently bound to long chain fatty acyl-CoAs. The protein catalyzes this reaction at low concentrations of magnesium chloride while the other enzyme has high activity at high concentrations of magnesium chloride.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Protein (UniProt):** Diacylglycerol O-acyltransferase 2 ([Q96PD7](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q96PD7/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Essential acyltransferase that catalyzes the terminal and only committed step in triacylglycerol synthesis by using diacylglycerol and fatty acyl CoA as substrates. Required for synthesis and storage of intracellular triglycerides. Probably plays a central role in cytosolic lipid accumulation. In liver, is primarily responsible for incorporating endogenously synthesized fatty acids into triglycerides (By similarity). Also functions as an acyl-CoA retinol acyltransferase (ARAT) (By similarity). Also able to use 1-monoalkylglycerol (1-MAkG) as an acyl acceptor for the synthesis of monoalkyl-monoacylglycerol (MAMAG).

**Pathway (UniProt):** Glycerolipid metabolism; triacylglycerol biosynthesis.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Endoplasmic reticulum membrane; Lipid droplet; Cytoplasm, perinuclear region.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Predominantly expressed in liver and white adipose tissue. Expressed at lower level in mammary gland, testis and peripheral blood leukocytes. Expressed in sebaceous glands of normal skin but decreased psoriatic skin.
