# SLC10A2: solute carrier family 10 member 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 13 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Encodes a sodium/bile acid cotransporter. This transporter is the primary mechanism for uptake of intestinal bile acids by apical cells in the distal ileum.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Ileal sodium/bile acid cotransporter ([Q12908](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q12908/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Plays a critical role in the sodium-dependent reabsorption of bile acids from the lumen of the small intestine. Transports various bile acids, unconjugated or conjugated, such as cholate and taurocholate. Also responsible for bile acid transport in the renal proximal tubules, a salvage mechanism that helps conserve bile acids (Probable). Works collaboratively with the Na(+)-taurocholate cotransporting polypeptide (NTCP), the organic solute transporter (OST), and the bile salt export pump (BSEP), to ensure efficacious biological recycling of bile acids during enterohepatic circulation.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Mainly expressed in ileum and kidney, lower expression in cecum.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Bile acid malabsorption, primary, 1 (PBAM1).
