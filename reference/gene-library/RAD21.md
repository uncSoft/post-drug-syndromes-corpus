# RAD21: RAD21 cohesin complex component

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (chromosome cohesion and architecture). The protein encoded by this gene is highly similar to the gene product of Schizosaccharomyces pombe rad21, a gene involved in the repair of DNA double-strand breaks, as well as in chromatid cohesion during mitosis. This protein is a nuclear phospho-protein, which becomes hyperphosphorylated in cell cycle M phase.

**Corpus relevance:** Sister-chromatid cohesion and genome architecture; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Double-strand-break repair protein rad21 homolog ([O60216](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O60216/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Double-strand-break repair protein rad21 homolog: As a member of the cohesin complex, involved in sister chromatid cohesion from the time of DNA replication in S phase to their segregation in mitosis, a function that is essential for proper chromosome segregation, post-replicative DNA repair, and the prevention of inappropriate recombination between repetitive regions. The cohesin complex may also play a role in spindle pole assembly during mitosis. In interphase, cohesins may function in the control of gene expression by binding to numerous sites within the genome (By similarity). May control RUNX1 gene expression (Probable). Binds to and represses APOB gene promoter. May play a role in embryonic gut development, possibly through the regulation of enteric neuron development (By similarity). 64-kDa C-terminal product: May promote apoptosis.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Nucleus matrix; Chromosome; Chromosome, centromere; Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton, spindle pole; Cytoplasm, cytosol.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in the gut (at protein level).

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Cornelia de Lange syndrome 4 with or without midline brain defects (CDLS4); Mungan syndrome (MGS).
