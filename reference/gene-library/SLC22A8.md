# SLC22A8: solute carrier family 22 member 8

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Encodes a protein involved in the sodium-independent transport and excretion of organic anions, some of which are potentially toxic. The protein is an integral membrane protein and appears to be localized to the basolateral membrane of the kidney.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Organic anion transporter 3 ([Q8TCC7](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8TCC7/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Functions as an organic anion/dicarboxylate exchanger that couples organic anion uptake indirectly to the sodium gradient. Transports organic anions such as estrone 3-sulfate (E1S) and urate in exchange for dicarboxylates such as glutarate or ketoglutarate (2-oxoglutarate). Plays an important role in the excretion of endogenous and exogenous organic anions, especially from the kidney and the brain. E1S transport is pH- and chloride-dependent and may also involve E1S/cGMP exchange. Responsible for the transport of prostaglandin E2 (PGE2) and prostaglandin F2(alpha) (PGF2(alpha)) in the basolateral side of the renal tubule. Involved in the transport of neuroactive tryptophan metabolites kynurenate and xanthurenate. Functions as a biopterin transporters involved in the uptake and the secretion of coenzymes tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4), dihydrobiopterin (BH2) and sepiapterin to urine, thereby determining baseline levels of blood biopterins.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Basolateral cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Strongly expressed in kidney. Weaker expression in brain and skeletal muscle. Expressed in adrenal glands.
