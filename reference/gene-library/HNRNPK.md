# HNRNPK: heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein K

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 9 (rna processing and mirna biogenesis). This gene belongs to the subfamily of ubiquitously expressed heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoproteins (hnRNPs). The hnRNPs are RNA binding proteins and they complex with heterogeneous nuclear RNA (hnRNA).

**Corpus relevance:** miRNA biogenesis and RNA handling; epigenetic-adjacent regulation with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein K ([P61978](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P61978/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** One of the major pre-mRNA-binding proteins. Binds tenaciously to poly(C) sequences. Likely to play a role in the nuclear metabolism of hnRNAs, particularly for pre-mRNAs that contain cytidine-rich sequences. Can also bind poly(C) single-stranded DNA. Plays an important role in p53/TP53 response to DNA damage, acting at the level of both transcription activation and repression. When sumoylated, acts as a transcriptional coactivator of p53/TP53, playing a role in p21/CDKN1A and 14-3-3 sigma/SFN induction. As far as transcription repression is concerned, acts by interacting with long intergenic RNA p21 (lincRNA-p21), a non-coding RNA induced by p53/TP53. This interaction is necessary for the induction of apoptosis, but not cell cycle arrest. As part of a ribonucleoprotein complex composed at least of ZNF827, HNRNPL and the circular RNA circZNF827 that nucleates the complex on chromatin, may negatively regulate the transcription of genes involved in neuronal differentiation.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Nucleus, nucleoplasm; Cell projection, podosome.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Au-Kline syndrome (AUKS).
