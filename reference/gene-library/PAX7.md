# PAX7: paired box 7

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 1 (cell signaling and transcription factors). This gene is a member of the paired box (PAX) family of transcription factors. Members of this gene family typically contain a paired box domain, an octapeptide, and a paired-type homeodomain.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Paired box protein Pax-7 ([P23759](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P23759/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transcription factor that is involved in the regulation of muscle stem cells proliferation, playing a role in myogenesis and muscle regeneration.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Rhabdomyosarcoma 2 (RMS2); Congenital myopathy 19 (CMYO19).
