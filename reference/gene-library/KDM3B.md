# KDM3B: lysine demethylase 3B

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 5 (epigenetic regulation). Predicted to enable chromatin DNA binding activity; histone H3K9 demethylase activity; and transcription coregulator activity. Predicted to be involved in regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Lysine-specific demethylase 3B ([Q7LBC6](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q7LBC6/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Histone demethylase that specifically demethylates 'Lys-9' of histone H3, thereby playing a central role in histone code. Demethylation of Lys residue generates formaldehyde and succinate. May have tumor suppressor activity.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous. Highly expressed in placenta, skeletal muscle, kidney, heart and liver.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Diets-Jongmans syndrome (DIJOS).
