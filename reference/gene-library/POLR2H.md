# POLR2H: RNA polymerase II, I and III subunit H

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (basal transcription machinery). The three eukaryotic RNA polymerases are complex multisubunit enzymes that play a central role in the transcription of nuclear genes. This gene encodes an essential and highly conserved subunit of RNA polymerase II that is shared by the other two eukaryotic DNA-directed RNA polymerases, I and III.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** DNA-directed RNA polymerases I, II, and III subunit RPABC3 ([P52434](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P52434/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** DNA-dependent RNA polymerase catalyzes the transcription of DNA into RNA using the four ribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates. Common component of RNA polymerases I, II and III which synthesize ribosomal RNA precursors, mRNA precursors and many functional non-coding RNAs, and small RNAs, such as 5S rRNA and tRNAs, respectively.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Nucleus, nucleolus.
