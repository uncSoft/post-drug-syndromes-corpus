# NOS3: nitric oxide synthase 3

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 7 (neuroendocrine and peptide signaling). Nitric oxide is a reactive free radical which acts as a biologic mediator in several processes, including neurotransmission and antimicrobial and antitumoral activities. Nitric oxide is synthesized from L-arginine by nitric oxide synthases.

**Corpus relevance:** Peptide-hormone and related signaling modulates sexual function, stress, and behavior, symptom domains prominent in PSSD/PFS.

**Protein (UniProt):** Nitric oxide synthase 3 ([P29474](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P29474/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Produces nitric oxide (NO) which is implicated in vascular smooth muscle relaxation through a cGMP-mediated signal transduction pathway. NO mediates vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF)-induced angiogenesis in coronary vessels and promotes blood clotting through the activation of platelets.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane; Membrane, caveola; Cytoplasm, cytoskeleton; Golgi apparatus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Platelets, placenta, liver and kidney.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Moyamoya disease 8 (MYMY8).
