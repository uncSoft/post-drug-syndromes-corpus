# GTF2A1: general transcription factor IIA subunit 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 14 (basal transcription machinery). Accurate transcription initiation on TATA-containing class II genes involves the ordered assembly of RNA polymerase II (POLR2A; MIM 180660) and several general initiation factors (summarized by DeJong and Roeder, 1993 ). One of these factors is TFIIA, which when purified from HeLa extracts consists of 35-, 19-, and 12-kD subunits.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Transcription initiation factor IIA subunit 1 ([P52655](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P52655/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** TFIIA is a component of the transcription machinery of RNA polymerase II and plays an important role in transcriptional activation. TFIIA in a complex with TBP mediates transcriptional activity.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.
