# DBH: dopamine beta-hydroxylase

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 9 (neurotransmission). The protein encoded by this gene is an oxidoreductase belonging to the copper type II, ascorbate-dependent monooxygenase family. The encoded protein, expressed in neuroscretory vesicles and chromaffin granules of the adrenal medulla, catalyzes the conversion of dopamine to norepinephrine, which functions as both a hormone and as the main neurotransmitter of the sympathetic nervous system.

**Corpus relevance:** Monoamine, GABA, glutamate, and cholinergic systems are the direct pharmacologic targets of SSRIs/SNRIs (PSSD) and the downstream effectors of neurosteroid signaling (PFS).

**Protein (UniProt):** Dopamine beta-hydroxylase ([P09172](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P09172/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Catalyzes the hydroxylation of dopamine to noradrenaline (also known as norepinephrine), and is thus vital for regulation of these neurotransmitters.

**Pathway (UniProt):** Catecholamine biosynthesis; (R)-noradrenaline biosynthesis; (R)-noradrenaline from dopamine: step 1/1.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasmic vesicle, secretory vesicle lumen; Cytoplasmic vesicle, secretory vesicle, chromaffin granule lumen; Secreted; Cytoplasmic vesicle, secretory vesicle membrane; Cytoplasmic vesicle, secretory vesicle, chromaffin granule membrane.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Orthostatic hypotension 1 (ORTHYP1).
