# SLC22A13: solute carrier family 22 member 13

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Encodes a member of the organic-cation transporter family. It is located in a gene cluster with another member of the family, organic cation transporter like 4.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Solute carrier family 22 member 13 ([Q9Y226](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9Y226/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Anion antiporter that mediates the transport of urate, orotate and nicotinate in exchange for organic or inorganic anions. Translocates urate and orotate across the apical membrane of proximal tubule epithelial cells and involved in urate renal reabsorption. Possibly involved in orotate renal reabsorption and nicotinate intestinal reabsorption. Mediates urate uptake by an exchange with organic anions such as (S)-lactate, succinate, glutathione and nicotinate. Urate and orotate transports are Cl(-)-dependent. Shows similar transport characteristics as the urate/orotate renal antiporter SLC22A12/URAT1 and may act as a compensator of SLC22A12/URAT1 in certain conditions (Probable).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Apical cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous. Highly expressed in kidneys and to a weaker extent in brain, heart, and intestine. In kidneys, expressed in proximal convoluted tubule. In kidneys, also expressed in cortical collecting duct, whereas glomerulus and thick ascending limb exhibit no expression.
