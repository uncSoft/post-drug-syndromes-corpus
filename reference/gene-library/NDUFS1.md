# NDUFS1: NADH:ubiquinone oxidoreductase core subunit S1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 2 (mitochondrial function and energy). The protein encoded by this gene belongs to the complex I 75 kDa subunit family. Mammalian complex I is composed of 45 different subunits.

**Corpus relevance:** No record in the library measures mitochondrial function or oxidative stress in people with these syndromes; the records that touch either are animal or cell work, and they point in mixed directions. These genes are here for their place in energy metabolism, not for an established link.

**Protein (UniProt):** NADH-ubiquinone oxidoreductase 75 kDa subunit, mitochondrial ([P28331](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P28331/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Core subunit of the mitochondrial membrane respiratory chain NADH dehydrogenase (Complex I) which catalyzes electron transfer from NADH through the respiratory chain, using ubiquinone as an electron acceptor. Essential for catalysing the entry and efficient transfer of electrons within complex I. Plays a key role in the assembly and stability of complex I and participates in the association of complex I with ubiquinol-cytochrome reductase complex (Complex III) to form supercomplexes.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion inner membrane.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Mitochondrial complex I deficiency, nuclear type 5 (MC1DN5).
