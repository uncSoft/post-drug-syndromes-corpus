# SLC22A14: solute carrier family 22 member 14

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Encodes a member of the organic-cation transporter family. It is located in a gene cluster with another member of the family, organic cation transporter like 3.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Solute carrier family 22 member 14 ([Q9Y267](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9Y267/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Riboflavin transporter localized at the inner mitochondrial membrane of the spermatozoa midpiece, which is required for male fertility (By similarity). SLC22A14-mediated riboflavin transport is essential for spermatozoa energy generation and motility: riboflavin is the precursor of FMN and FAD, which are coenzymes of many enzymes in the TCA cycle (the citric acid cycle) in mitochondria (By similarity). Required for sperm motility and normal sperm flagellar structure (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion inner membrane; Cell projection, cilium, flagellum membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous.
