# HTR3C: 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor 3C

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (neurotransmission). The product of this gene belongs to the ligand-gated ion channel receptor superfamily. This gene encodes subunit C of the type 3 receptor for 5-hydroxytryptamine (serotonin), a biogenic hormone that functions as a neurotransmitter, a hormone, and a mitogen.

**Corpus relevance:** Monoamine, GABA, glutamate, and cholinergic systems are the direct pharmacologic targets of SSRIs/SNRIs (PSSD) and the downstream effectors of neurosteroid signaling (PFS).

**Protein (UniProt):** 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor 3C ([Q8WXA8](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8WXA8/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Forms serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine/5-HT3)-activated cation-selective channel complexes, which when activated cause fast, depolarizing responses in neurons.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Postsynaptic cell membrane; Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in many tissues including adult brain, colon, intestine, lung, muscle and stomach as well as fetal colon and kidney.
