# PRLR: prolactin receptor

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 5 (hpg and gonadal axis). Encodes a receptor for the anterior pituitary hormone prolactin, and belongs to the type I cytokine receptor family. Prolactin-dependent signaling occurs as the result of ligand-induced dimerization of the prolactin receptor.

**Corpus relevance:** HPG-axis signaling and steroid carrier proteins govern testosterone production and bioavailability. Whether this axis is disturbed in post-drug syndromes is unsettled: the library holds no LH or FSH measurement in men with persistent symptoms after finasteride or an SSRI, average testosterone and DHT were normal in the controlled study (SIDE-010), and 13% of 24 men in a smaller series had confirmed low levels of both (SIDE-008).

**Protein (UniProt):** Prolactin receptor ([P16471](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P16471/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** This is a receptor for the anterior pituitary hormone prolactin (PRL). Acts as a prosurvival factor for spermatozoa by inhibiting sperm capacitation through suppression of SRC kinase activation and stimulation of AKT. Isoform 4 is unable to transduce prolactin signaling. Isoform 6 is unable to transduce prolactin signaling.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Membrane; Secreted.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in breast, placenta, kidney, liver and pancreas.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Multiple fibroadenomas of the breast (MFAB); Hyperprolactinemia (HPRL).
