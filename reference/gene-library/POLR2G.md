# POLR2G: RNA polymerase II subunit G

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (basal transcription machinery). Encodes the seventh largest subunit of RNA polymerase II, the polymerase responsible for synthesizing messenger RNA in eukaryotes. The protein functions in transcription initiation, and is also thought to help stabilize transcribing polyermase molecules during elongation.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** DNA-directed RNA polymerase II subunit RPB7 ([P62487](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P62487/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Core component of RNA polymerase II (Pol II), a DNA-dependent RNA polymerase which synthesizes mRNA precursors and many functional non-coding RNAs using the four ribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates. Pol II is the central component of the basal RNA polymerase II transcription machinery. It is composed of mobile elements that move relative to each other. POLR2G/RPB7 is part of a subcomplex with POLR2D/RPB4 that binds to a pocket formed by POLR2A/RPB1, POLR2B/RPB2 and POLR2F/RPABC2 at the base of the clamp element. The POLR2D/RPB4-POLR2G/RPB7 subcomplex seems to lock the clamp via POLR2G/RPB7 in the closed conformation thus preventing double-stranded DNA to enter the active site cleft. The POLR2D/RPB4-POLR2G/RPB7 subcomplex binds single-stranded DNA and RNA.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.
