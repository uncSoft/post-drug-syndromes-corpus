# CHD6: chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 6

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 20 (epigenetic regulation). Encodes a member of the SNF2/RAD54 helicase protein family. The protein contains two chromodomains, a helicase domain, and an ATPase domain.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler CHD6 ([Q8TD26](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q8TD26/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin-remodeling factor. Regulates transcription by disrupting nucleosomes in a largely non-sliding manner which strongly increases the accessibility of chromatin; nucleosome disruption requires ATP. Activates transcription of specific genes in response to oxidative stress through interaction with NFE2L2.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus, nucleoplasm.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Widely expressed.
