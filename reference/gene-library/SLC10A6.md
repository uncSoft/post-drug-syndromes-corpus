# SLC10A6: solute carrier family 10 member 6

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 4 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Predicted to enable bile acid:sodium symporter activity. Predicted to be involved in bile acid and bile salt transport.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Sodium-dependent organic anion transporter ([Q3KNW5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q3KNW5/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transports sulfoconjugated steroid hormones from the extracellular compartment into the cytosol in a sodium-dependent manner without hydrolysis. Steroid sulfate hormones are commonly considered to be biologically inactive metabolites, that may be activated by steroid sulfatases into free steroids. May play an important role by delivering sulfoconjugated steroids to specific target cells in reproductive organs (By similarity). May play a role transporting the estriol precursor 16alpha-hydroxydehydroepiandrosterone 3-sulfate (16a-OH-DHEAS) at the fetal blood vessel endothelium. Can also transport other sulfoconjugated molecules such as taurolithocholic acid-3-sulfate and sulfoconjugated pyrenes.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Highly expressed in testis, placenta and pancreas. Moderately expressed in heart, lung and mammary gland. Weakly expressed in brain, colon, kidney, liver, ovary, prostate, small intestine, spleen and thymus.
