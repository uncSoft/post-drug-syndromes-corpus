# STARD5: StAR related lipid transfer domain containing 5

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 15 (steroid hormone synthesis). Proteins containing a steroidogenic acute regulatory-related lipid transfer (START) domain are often involved in the trafficking of lipids and cholesterol between diverse intracellular membranes. This gene is a member of the StarD subfamily that encodes START-related lipid transfer proteins.

**Corpus relevance:** Steroid-synthesis enzymes sit directly on the pathways perturbed by 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors and SSRIs; inherited variation here is a candidate susceptibility factor for persistent post-drug phenotypes.

**Protein (UniProt):** StAR-related lipid transfer protein 5 ([Q9NSY2](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9NSY2/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** May be involved in the intracellular transport of sterols or other lipids. May bind cholesterol or other sterols (By similarity).
