# GATA3: GATA binding protein 3

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 10 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Encodes a protein which belongs to the GATA family of transcription factors. The protein contains two GATA-type zinc fingers and is an important regulator of T-cell development and plays an important role in endothelial cell biology.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Trans-acting T-cell-specific transcription factor GATA-3 ([P23771](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P23771/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transcriptional activator which binds to the enhancer of the T-cell receptor alpha and delta genes. Binds to the consensus sequence 5'-AGATAG-3'. Required for the T-helper 2 (Th2) differentiation process following immune and inflammatory responses. Positively regulates ASB2 expression (By similarity). Coordinates macrophage transcriptional activation and UCP2-dependent metabolic reprogramming in response to IL33. Upon tissue injury, acts downstream of IL33 signaling to drive differentiation of inflammation-resolving alternatively activated macrophages.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** T-cells and endothelial cells.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypoparathyroidism, sensorineural deafness, and renal disease (HDR).
