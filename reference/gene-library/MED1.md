# MED1: mediator complex subunit 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 17 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Encodes a subunit of the CRSP (cofactor required for SP1 activation) complex, which, along with TFIID, is required for efficient activation by SP1. The protein is also a component of thyroid hormone receptor-associated complexes, regulates p53-dependent apoptosis and is essential for adipogenesis.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Mediator of RNA polymerase II transcription subunit 1 ([Q15648](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q15648/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Component of the Mediator complex, a coactivator involved in the regulated transcription of nearly all RNA polymerase II-dependent genes. Mediator functions as a bridge to convey information from gene-specific regulatory proteins to the basal RNA polymerase II transcription machinery. Mediator is recruited to promoters by direct interactions with regulatory proteins and serves as a scaffold for the assembly of a functional preinitiation complex with RNA polymerase II and the general transcription factors. Acts as a coactivator for GATA1-mediated transcriptional activation during erythroid differentiation of K562 erythroleukemia cells.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitously expressed.
