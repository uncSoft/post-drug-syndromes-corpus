# HTR3A: 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor 3A

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (neurotransmission). The product of this gene belongs to the ligand-gated ion channel receptor superfamily. This gene encodes subunit A of the type 3 receptor for 5-hydroxytryptamine (serotonin), a biogenic hormone that functions as a neurotransmitter, a hormone, and a mitogen.

**Corpus relevance:** Monoamine, GABA, glutamate, and cholinergic systems are the direct pharmacologic targets of SSRIs/SNRIs (PSSD) and the downstream effectors of neurosteroid signaling (PFS).

**Protein (UniProt):** 5-hydroxytryptamine receptor 3A ([P46098](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P46098/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Forms serotonin (5-hydroxytryptamine/5-HT3)-activated cation-selective channel complexes, which when activated cause fast, depolarizing responses in neurons.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Postsynaptic cell membrane; Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in cerebral cortex, amygdala, hippocampus, and testis. Detected in monocytes of the spleen and tonsil, in small and large intestine, uterus, prostate, ovary and placenta.
