# NCOR2: nuclear receptor corepressor 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 12 (steroid and nuclear hormone receptors). Encodes a nuclear receptor co-repressor that mediates transcriptional silencing of certain target genes. The protein is a member of a family of thyroid hormone- and retinoic acid receptor-associated co-repressors.

**Corpus relevance:** Nuclear receptors transduce steroid signals into lasting gene-expression programs; persistent receptor-level remodeling (or silencing) is a leading hypothesis for symptoms that outlast drug exposure.

**Protein (UniProt):** Nuclear receptor corepressor 2 ([Q9Y618](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9Y618/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transcriptional corepressor that mediates the transcriptional repression activity of some nuclear receptors by promoting chromatin condensation, thus preventing access of the basal transcription. Acts by recruiting chromatin modifiers, such as histone deacetylases HDAC1, HDAC2 and HDAC3. Required to activate the histone deacetylase activity of HDAC3. Involved in the regulation BCL6-dependent of the germinal center (GC) reactions, mainly through the control of the GC B-cells proliferation and survival. Recruited by ZBTB7A to the androgen response elements/ARE on target genes, negatively regulates androgen receptor signaling and androgen-induced cell proliferation.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous. High levels of expression are detected in lung, spleen and brain.
