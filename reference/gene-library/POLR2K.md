# POLR2K: RNA polymerase II, I and III subunit K

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (basal transcription machinery). Encodes one of the smallest subunits of RNA polymerase II, the polymerase responsible for synthesizing messenger RNA in eukaryotes. This subunit is shared by the other two DNA-directed RNA polymerases.

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** DNA-directed RNA polymerases I, II, and III subunit RPABC4 ([P53803](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P53803/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** DNA-dependent RNA polymerase catalyzes the transcription of DNA into RNA using the four ribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates. Common component of RNA polymerases I, II and III which synthesize ribosomal RNA precursors, mRNA precursors and many functional non-coding RNAs, and a small RNAs, such as 5S rRNA and tRNAs, respectively.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Nucleus, nucleolus.
