# CHD9: chromodomain helicase DNA binding protein 9

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 16 (epigenetic regulation). Predicted to enable ATP binding activity; ATP hydrolysis activity; and DNA binding activity. Predicted to be involved in DNA-templated transcription.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-dependent chromatin remodeler CHD9 ([Q3L8U1](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q3L8U1/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Probable ATP-dependent chromatin-remodeling factor. Acts as a transcriptional coactivator for PPARA and possibly other nuclear receptors. Has DNA-dependent ATPase activity and binds to A/T-rich DNA. Associates with A/T-rich regulatory regions in promoters of genes that participate in the differentiation of progenitors during osteogenesis (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Widely expressed at low levels. In bone marrow, expression is restricted to osteoprogenitor cells adjacent to mature osteoblasts.
