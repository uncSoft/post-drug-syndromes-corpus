# PDE5A: phosphodiesterase 5A

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 4 (neuroendocrine and peptide signaling). Encodes a cGMP-binding, cGMP-specific phosphodiesterase, a member of the cyclic nucleotide phosphodiesterase family. This phosphodiesterase specifically hydrolyzes cGMP to 5'-GMP.

**Corpus relevance:** Peptide-hormone and related signaling modulates sexual function, stress, and behavior, symptom domains prominent in PSSD/PFS.

**Protein (UniProt):** cGMP-specific 3',5'-cyclic phosphodiesterase ([O76074](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O76074/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Plays a role in signal transduction by regulating the intracellular concentration of cyclic nucleotides. This phosphodiesterase catalyzes the specific hydrolysis of cGMP to 5'-GMP. Specifically regulates nitric-oxide-generated cGMP.

**Pathway (UniProt):** Purine metabolism; 3',5'-cyclic GMP degradation; GMP from 3',5'-cyclic GMP: step 1/1.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in aortic smooth muscle cells, heart, placenta, skeletal muscle and pancreas and, to a much lesser extent, in brain, liver and lung.
