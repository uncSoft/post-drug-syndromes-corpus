# MED25: mediator complex subunit 25

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 19 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Encodes a component of the transcriptional coactivator complex termed the Mediator complex. This complex is required for transcription of most RNA polymerase II-dependent genes.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Mediator of RNA polymerase II transcription subunit 25 ([Q71SY5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q71SY5/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Component of the Mediator complex, a coactivator involved in the regulated transcription of nearly all RNA polymerase II-dependent genes. Mediator functions as a bridge to convey information from gene-specific regulatory proteins to the basal RNA polymerase II transcription machinery. Mediator is recruited to promoters by direct interactions with regulatory proteins and serves as a scaffold for the assembly of a functional preinitiation complex with RNA polymerase II and the general transcription factors. Required for RARA/RXRA-mediated transcription.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitously expressed. Highest levels in brain, heart, kidney, peripheral leukocytes, placenta, skeletal muscle and spleen.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease, axonal, type 2B2 (CMT2B2); Basel-Vanagaite-Smirin-Yosef syndrome (BVSYS).
