# FABP7: fatty acid binding protein 7

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 6 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). The gene encodes a small, highly conserved cytoplasmic protein that bind long-chain fatty acids and other hydrophobic ligands. The protein is important in the establishment of the radial glial fiber in the developing brain.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Protein (UniProt):** Fatty acid-binding protein, brain ([O15540](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O15540/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** B-FABP could be involved in the transport of a so far unknown hydrophobic ligand with potential morphogenic activity during CNS development. It is required for the establishment of the radial glial fiber system in developing brain, a system that is necessary for the migration of immature neurons to establish cortical layers (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in brain and other neural tissues.
