# INO80C: INO80 complex subunit C

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 18 (epigenetic regulation). Involved in several processes, including positive regulation of DNA-templated transcription; regulation of DNA metabolic process; and regulation of chromosome organization. Located in fibrillar center and nucleoplasm.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** INO80 complex subunit C ([Q6PI98](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q6PI98/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Proposed core component of the chromatin remodeling INO80 complex which is involved in transcriptional regulation, DNA replication and probably DNA repair.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.
