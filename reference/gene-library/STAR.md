# STAR: steroidogenic acute regulatory protein

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (steroid hormone synthesis). The protein encoded by this gene plays a key role in the acute regulation of steroid hormone synthesis by enhancing the conversion of cholesterol into pregnenolone. This protein permits the cleavage of cholesterol into pregnenolone by mediating the transport of cholesterol from the outer mitochondrial membrane to the inner mitochondrial membrane.

**Corpus relevance:** Steroid-synthesis enzymes sit directly on the pathways perturbed by 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors and SSRIs; inherited variation here is a candidate susceptibility factor for persistent post-drug phenotypes.

**Protein (UniProt):** Steroidogenic acute regulatory protein, mitochondrial ([P49675](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P49675/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Plays a key role in steroid hormone synthesis by enhancing the metabolism of cholesterol into pregnenolone. Mediates the transfer of cholesterol from the outer mitochondrial membrane to the inner mitochondrial membrane where it is cleaved to pregnenolone.

**Pathway (UniProt):** Steroid metabolism; cholesterol metabolism.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion outer membrane; Mitochondrion matrix.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in gonads, adrenal cortex and kidney.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Adrenal hyperplasia 1 (AH1).
