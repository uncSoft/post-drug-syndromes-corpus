# POLG: DNA polymerase gamma, catalytic subunit

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 15 (mitochondrial function and energy). Mitochondrial DNA polymerase is heterotrimeric, consisting of a homodimer of accessory subunits plus a catalytic subunit. The protein encoded by this gene is the catalytic subunit of mitochondrial DNA polymerase.

**Corpus relevance:** No record in the library measures mitochondrial function or oxidative stress in people with these syndromes; the records that touch either are animal or cell work, and they point in mixed directions. These genes are here for their place in energy metabolism, not for an established link.

**Protein (UniProt):** DNA polymerase subunit gamma-1 ([P54098](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P54098/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Catalytic subunit of DNA polymerase gamma solely responsible for replication of mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA). Replicates both heavy and light strands of the circular mtDNA genome using a single-stranded DNA template, RNA primers and the four deoxyribonucleoside triphosphates as substrates. Has 5' -> 3' polymerase activity. Functionally interacts with TWNK and SSBP1 at the replication fork to form a highly processive replisome, where TWNK unwinds the double-stranded DNA template prior to replication and SSBP1 covers the parental heavy strand to enable continuous replication of the entire mitochondrial genome. A single nucleotide incorporation cycle includes binding of the incoming nucleotide at the insertion site, a phosphodiester bond formation reaction that extends the 3'-end of the primer DNA, and translocation of the primer terminus to the post-insertion site.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion; Mitochondrion matrix, mitochondrion nucleoid.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Progressive external ophthalmoplegia with mitochondrial DNA deletions, autosomal dominant, 1 (PEOA1); Progressive external ophthalmoplegia with mitochondrial DNA deletions, autosomal recessive, 1 (PEOB1); Sensory ataxic neuropathy dysarthria and ophthalmoparesis (SANDO); Mitochondrial DNA depletion syndrome 4A (MTDPS4A); Mitochondrial DNA depletion syndrome 4B (MTDPS4B); Leigh syndrome (LS); Spinocerebellar ataxia with epilepsy (SCAE).
