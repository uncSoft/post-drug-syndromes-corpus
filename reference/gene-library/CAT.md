# CAT: catalase

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (mitochondrial function and energy). Encodes catalase, a key antioxidant enzyme in the bodies defense against oxidative stress. Catalase is a heme enzyme that is present in the peroxisome of nearly all aerobic cells.

**Corpus relevance:** No record in the library measures mitochondrial function or oxidative stress in people with these syndromes; the records that touch either are animal or cell work, and they point in mixed directions. These genes are here for their place in energy metabolism, not for an established link.

**Protein (UniProt):** Catalase ([P04040](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04040/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Catalyzes the degradation of hydrogen peroxide (H(2)O(2)) generated by peroxisomal oxidases to water and oxygen, thereby protecting cells from the toxic effects of hydrogen peroxide. Promotes growth of cells including T-cells, B-cells, myeloid leukemia cells, melanoma cells, mastocytoma cells and normal and transformed fibroblast cells.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Peroxisome matrix.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Acatalasemia (ACATLAS).
