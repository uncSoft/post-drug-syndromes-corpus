# AVPR2: arginine vasopressin receptor 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome X (neuroendocrine and peptide signaling). Encodes the vasopressin receptor, type 2, also known as the V2 receptor, which belongs to the seven-transmembrane-domain G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) superfamily, and couples to Gs thus stimulating adenylate cyclase. The subfamily that includes the V2 receptor, the V1a and V1b vasopressin receptors, the oxytocin receptor, and isotocin and mesotocin receptors in non-mammals, is well conserved, though several members signal via other G proteins.

**Corpus relevance:** Peptide-hormone and related signaling modulates sexual function, stress, and behavior, symptom domains prominent in PSSD/PFS.

**Protein (UniProt):** Vasopressin V2 receptor ([P30518](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P30518/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** G protein-coupled receptor for arginine vasopressin, an antidiuretic that promotes renal water reabsorption. Ligand binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling via guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the activity of downstream effectors, such as adenylate cyclase (cAMP). AVPR2 is coupled to G(s) G alpha proteins and mediates activation of adenylate cyclase activity.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Kidney.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Nephrogenic syndrome of inappropriate antidiuresis (NSIAD); Diabetes insipidus, nephrogenic, 1, X-linked (NDI1).
