# SMC3: structural maintenance of chromosomes 3

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 10 (chromosome cohesion and architecture). This gene belongs to the SMC3 subfamily of SMC proteins. The protein occurs in certain cell types as either an intracellular, nuclear protein or a secreted protein.

**Corpus relevance:** Sister-chromatid cohesion and genome architecture; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Structural maintenance of chromosomes protein 3 ([Q9UQE7](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UQE7/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Central component of cohesin, a complex required for chromosome cohesion during the cell cycle. The cohesin complex may form a large proteinaceous ring within which sister chromatids can be trapped. At anaphase, the complex is cleaved and dissociates from chromatin, allowing sister chromatids to segregate. Cohesion is coupled to DNA replication and is involved in DNA repair. The cohesin complex also plays an important role in spindle pole assembly during mitosis and in chromosomes movement.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Chromosome; Chromosome, centromere.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Cornelia de Lange syndrome 3 with or without midline brain defects (CDLS3).
