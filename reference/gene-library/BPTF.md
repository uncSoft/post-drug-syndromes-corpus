# BPTF: bromodomain PHD finger transcription factor

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 17 (epigenetic regulation). This gene was identified by the reactivity of its encoded protein to a monoclonal antibody prepared against brain homogenates from patients with Alzheimer's disease. Analysis of the original protein (fetal Alz-50 reactive clone 1, or FAC1), identified as an 810 aa protein containing a DNA-binding domain and a zinc finger motif, suggested it might play a role in the regulation of transcription.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Nucleosome-remodeling factor subunit BPTF ([Q12830](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q12830/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Regulatory subunit of the ATP-dependent NURF-1 and NURF-5 ISWI chromatin remodeling complexes, which form ordered nucleosome arrays on chromatin and facilitate access to DNA during DNA-templated processes such as DNA replication, transcription, and repair. The NURF-1 ISWI chromatin remodeling complex has a lower ATP hydrolysis rate than the NURF-5 ISWI chromatin remodeling complex. Within the NURF-1 ISWI chromatin-remodeling complex, binds to the promoters of En1 and En2 to positively regulate their expression and promote brain development. Histone-binding protein which binds to H3 tails trimethylated on 'Lys-4' (H3K4me3), which mark transcription start sites of active genes. Binds to histone H3 tails dimethylated on 'Lys-4' (H3K4Me2) to a lesser extent. May also regulate transcription through direct binding to DNA or transcription factors.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitously expressed, with highest levels in testis. Present in kidney, liver and brain. In the brain, highest levels are found in motor cortex (at protein level).

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Neurodevelopmental disorder with dysmorphic facies and distal limb anomalies (NEDDFL).
