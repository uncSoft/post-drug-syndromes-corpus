# NPY2R: neuropeptide Y receptor Y2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 4 (neuroendocrine and peptide signaling). Enables G-protein alpha-subunit binding activity and peptide YY receptor activity. Involved in adenylate cyclase-inhibiting G protein-coupled receptor signaling pathway; cardiac left ventricle morphogenesis; and outflow tract morphogenesis.

**Corpus relevance:** Peptide-hormone and related signaling modulates sexual function, stress, and behavior, symptom domains prominent in PSSD/PFS.

**Protein (UniProt):** Neuropeptide Y receptor type 2 ([P49146](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P49146/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Receptor for neuropeptide Y (NPY) and peptide YY (PYY). Can also bind with lower affinity pancreatic polypeptide (PP/PPY) and [Leu-31, Pro-34]NPY, an analog of NPY modified at residues 31 and 34 of the mature form. NPY or PYY binding induces adenylate cyclase-inhibiting G protein-coupled receptor signaling, resulting in inhibition of cAMP production. Receptor activation by ligands also leads to an increase of intracellular Ca(2+) levels. Involved in the regulation of colonic antisecretory tone, and colonic contractility (By similarity). As receptor for peptide PYY(3-36) in the arcuate nucleus of the hypothalamus, it is involved in the down-regulation of appetite and food intake (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** High levels in amygdala, corpus callosum, hippocampus and subthalamic nucleus. Also detectable in caudate nucleus, hypothalamus and substantia nigra.
