# BRPF1: bromodomain and PHD finger containing 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 3 (epigenetic regulation). Encodes a bromodomain, PHD finger and chromo/Tudor-related Pro-Trp-Trp-Pro (PWWP) domain containing protein. The protein is a component of the MOZ/MORF histone acetyltransferase complexes which function as a transcriptional regulators.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Peregrin ([P55201](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P55201/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Scaffold subunit of various histone acetyltransferase (HAT) complexes, such as the MOZ/MORF and HBO1 complexes, which have a histone H3 acetyltransferase activity. Plays a key role in HBO1 complex by directing KAT7/HBO1 specificity towards histone H3 'Lys-14' acetylation (H3K14ac). Some HAT complexes preferentially mediate histone H3 'Lys-23' (H3K23ac) acetylation. Positively regulates the transcription of RUNX1 and RUNX2.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Chromosome; Cytoplasm.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** High levels in testis.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Intellectual developmental disorder with dysmorphic facies and ptosis (IDDDFP).
