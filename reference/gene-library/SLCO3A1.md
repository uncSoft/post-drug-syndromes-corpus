# SLCO3A1: solute carrier organic anion transporter family member 3A1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 15 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Enables prostaglandin transmembrane transporter activity. Involved in carboxylic acid transport and positive regulation of intracellular signal transduction.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Solute carrier organic anion transporter family member 3A1 ([Q9UIG8](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UIG8/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Putative organic anion antiporter with apparent broad substrate specificity. Recognizes various substrates including thyroid hormone L-thyroxine, prostanoids such as prostaglandin E1 and E2, bile acids such as taurocholate, glycolate and glycochenodeoxycholate and peptide hormones such as L-arginine vasopressin, likely operating in a tissue-specific manner. The transport mechanism, its electrogenicity and potential tissue-specific counterions remain to be elucidated (Probable). Mediates transport of tryptophan, phenyalanine and tyrosine.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Basolateral cell membrane; Apical cell membrane; Basal cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Generally the expression of isoform 1 is higher than that of isoform 2.
