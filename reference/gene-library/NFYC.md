# NFYC: nuclear transcription factor Y subunit gamma

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 1 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Encodes one subunit of a trimeric complex forming a highly conserved transcription factor that binds with high specificity to CCAAT motifs in the promoters of a variety of genes. The encoded protein, subunit C, forms a tight dimer with the B subunit, a prerequisite for subunit A association.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Nuclear transcription factor Y subunit gamma ([Q13952](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q13952/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Component of the sequence-specific heterotrimeric transcription factor (NF-Y) which specifically recognizes a 5'-CCAAT-3' box motif found in the promoters of its target genes. NF-Y can function as both an activator and a repressor, depending on its interacting cofactors.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.
