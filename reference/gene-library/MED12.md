# MED12: mediator complex subunit 12

A protein-coding gene on chromosome X (cell signaling and transcription factors). The initiation of transcription is controlled in part by a large protein assembly known as the preinitiation complex. A component of this preinitiation complex is a 1.2 MDa protein aggregate called Mediator.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Mediator of RNA polymerase II transcription subunit 12 ([Q93074](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q93074/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Component of the Mediator complex, a coactivator involved in the regulated transcription of nearly all RNA polymerase II-dependent genes. Mediator functions as a bridge to convey information from gene-specific regulatory proteins to the basal RNA polymerase II transcription machinery. Mediator is recruited to promoters by direct interactions with regulatory proteins and serves as a scaffold for the assembly of a functional pre-initiation complex with RNA polymerase II and the general transcription factors. This subunit may specifically regulate transcription of targets of the Wnt signaling pathway and SHH signaling pathway.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Opitz-Kaveggia syndrome (OKS); Intellectual developmental disorder, X-linked, syndromic, Lujan-Fryns type (MRXSLF); Ohdo syndrome, X-linked (OHDOX); Hardikar syndrome (HDKR).
