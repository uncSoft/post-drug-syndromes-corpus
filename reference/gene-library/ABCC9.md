# ABCC9: ATP binding cassette subfamily C member 9

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 12 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The protein encoded by this gene is a member of the superfamily of ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters. ABC proteins transport various molecules across extra- and intra-cellular membranes.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Powers note (his unpublished theorizing, research only, not medical advice):** Powers named the ABCC transporter family (2026 summit interview, unpublished) as intracellular transporter genes implicated in metabolite clearance in his experimentally verifiable mechanism; individual ABCC members not separately named.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-binding cassette sub-family C member 9 ([O60706](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/O60706/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Subunit of ATP-sensitive potassium channels (KATP). Can form cardiac and smooth muscle-type KATP channels with KCNJ11. KCNJ11 forms the channel pore while ABCC9 is required for activation and regulation. Can form a sulfonylurea-sensitive but ATP-insensitive potassium channel with KCNJ8 (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Membrane.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Cardiomyopathy, dilated, 1O (CMD1O); Atrial fibrillation, familial, 12 (ATFB12); Hypertrichotic osteochondrodysplasia (HTOCD); Intellectual disability and myopathy syndrome (IDMYS).
