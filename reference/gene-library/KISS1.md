# KISS1: KiSS-1 metastasis suppressor

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 1 (hpg and gonadal axis). This gene is a metastasis suppressor gene that suppresses metastases of melanomas and breast carcinomas without affecting tumorigenicity. The protein may inhibit chemotaxis and invasion and thereby attenuate metastasis in malignant melanomas.

**Corpus relevance:** HPG-axis signaling and steroid carrier proteins govern testosterone production and bioavailability. Whether this axis is disturbed in post-drug syndromes is unsettled: the library holds no LH or FSH measurement in men with persistent symptoms after finasteride or an SSRI, average testosterone and DHT were normal in the controlled study (SIDE-010), and 13% of 24 men in a smaller series had confirmed low levels of both (SIDE-008).

**Protein (UniProt):** Metastasis-suppressor KiSS-1 ([Q15726](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q15726/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Kisspeptins are ligands for the G protein-coupled receptor KISS1R/GPR54. The hypothalamic KISS1/KISS1R signaling system plays a central role in the regulation of the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal reproductive axis by modulating the secretion of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) from GnRH neurons. In these neurons, kisspeptin binding to its receptor activates G(q)-dependent signaling, leading to phospholipase C (PLC) activation, and hydrolysis of phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate (PIP2). The subsequent rise in intracellular calcium levels results in the inhibition of inward rectifier potassium channels and activation of TRPC-like cation channels, leading to GnRH neurons depolarization and stimulation (By similarity). In addition to this pathway, kisspeptin also triggers G(q)-independent signaling via beta-arrestin, leading to MAPK cascade activation and ERK1/ERK2 phosphorylation.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Secreted.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** High expression in placenta. Expression levels increased in both early placentas and molar pregnancies and are reduced in choriocarcinoma cells. In first trimester trophoblasts is expressed at higher levels than at term of gestation, but only expressed in the villous trophoblast. Also expressed in testis, pancreas, liver, small intestine.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism 13 with or without anosmia (HH13).
