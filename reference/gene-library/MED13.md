# MED13: mediator complex subunit 13

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 17 (cell signaling and transcription factors). Encodes a component of the mediator complex (also known as TRAP, SMCC, DRIP, or ARC), a transcriptional coactivator complex thought to be required for the expression of almost all genes. The mediator complex is recruited by transcriptional activators or nuclear receptors to induce gene expression, possibly by interacting with RNA polymerase II and promoting the formation of a transcriptional pre-initiation complex.

**Corpus relevance:** General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Mediator of RNA polymerase II transcription subunit 13 ([Q9UHV7](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UHV7/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Component of the Mediator complex, a coactivator involved in the regulated transcription of nearly all RNA polymerase II-dependent genes. Mediator functions as a bridge to convey information from gene-specific regulatory proteins to the basal RNA polymerase II transcription machinery. Mediator is recruited to promoters by direct interactions with regulatory proteins and serves as a scaffold for the assembly of a functional preinitiation complex with RNA polymerase II and the general transcription factors.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal dominant 61 (MRD61).
