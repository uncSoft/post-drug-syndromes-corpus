# GNRHR: gonadotropin releasing hormone receptor

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 4 (hpg and gonadal axis). Encodes the receptor for type 1 gonadotropin-releasing hormone. This receptor is a member of the seven-transmembrane, G-protein coupled receptor (GPCR) family.

**Corpus relevance:** HPG-axis signaling and steroid carrier proteins govern testosterone production and bioavailability. Whether this axis is disturbed in post-drug syndromes is unsettled: the library holds no LH or FSH measurement in men with persistent symptoms after finasteride or an SSRI, average testosterone and DHT were normal in the controlled study (SIDE-010), and 13% of 24 men in a smaller series had confirmed low levels of both (SIDE-008).

**Protein (UniProt):** Gonadotropin-releasing hormone receptor ([P30968](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P30968/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Receptor for gonadotropin releasing hormone (GnRH) that mediates the action of GnRH to stimulate the secretion of the gonadotropic hormones luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH). This receptor mediates its action by association with G-proteins that activate a phosphatidylinositol-calcium second messenger system. Isoform 2 may act as an inhibitor of GnRH-R signaling.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Pituitary, ovary, testis, breast and prostate but not in liver and spleen.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism 7 with or without anosmia (HH7).
