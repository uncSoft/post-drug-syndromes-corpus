# TAC3: tachykinin precursor 3

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 12 (hpg and gonadal axis). Encodes a member of the tachykinin family of secreted neuropeptides. The encoded preproprotein is proteolytically processed to generate the mature peptide, which is primarily expressed in the central and peripheral nervous systems and functions as a neurotransmitter.

**Corpus relevance:** HPG-axis signaling and steroid carrier proteins govern testosterone production and bioavailability. Whether this axis is disturbed in post-drug syndromes is unsettled: the library holds no LH or FSH measurement in men with persistent symptoms after finasteride or an SSRI, average testosterone and DHT were normal in the controlled study (SIDE-010), and 13% of 24 men in a smaller series had confirmed low levels of both (SIDE-008).

**Protein (UniProt):** Tachykinin-3 ([Q9UHF0](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UHF0/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Tachykinins are active peptides which excite neurons, evoke behavioral responses, are potent vasodilators and secretagogues, and contract (directly or indirectly) many smooth muscles.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Secreted.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism 10 with or without anosmia (HH10).
