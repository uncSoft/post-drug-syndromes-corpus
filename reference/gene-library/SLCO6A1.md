# SLCO6A1: solute carrier organic anion transporter family member 6A1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 5 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). Predicted to be involved in sodium-independent organic anion transport. Predicted to be located in sperm principal piece.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Solute carrier organic anion transporter family member 6A1 ([Q86UG4](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q86UG4/entry), reviewed)

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Strongly expressed in testis. Weakly expressed in spleen, brain, fetal brain and placenta. Detected in lung tumors.
