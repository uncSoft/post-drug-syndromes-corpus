# SUZ12: SUZ12 polycomb repressive complex 2 subunit

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 17 (epigenetic regulation). This zinc finger gene has been identified at the breakpoints of a recurrent chromosomal translocation reported in endometrial stromal sarcoma. Recombination of these breakpoints results in the fusion of this gene and JAZF1.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Polycomb protein SUZ12 ([Q15022](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q15022/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Polycomb group (PcG) protein. Component of the PRC2 complex, which methylates 'Lys-9' (H3K9me) and 'Lys-27' (H3K27me) of histone H3, leading to transcriptional repression of the affected target gene. The PRC2 complex may also serve as a recruiting platform for DNA methyltransferases, thereby linking two epigenetic repression systems. Genes repressed by the PRC2 complex include HOXC8, HOXA9, MYT1 and CDKN2A.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Overexpressed in breast and colon cancer.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Imagawa-Matsumoto syndrome (IMMAS).
