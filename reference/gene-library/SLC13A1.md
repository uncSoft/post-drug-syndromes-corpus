# SLC13A1: solute carrier family 13 member 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 7 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The protein encoded by this gene is an apical membrane Na(+)-sulfate cotransporter involved in sulfate homeostasis in the kidney. Defects in this gene lead to many pathophysiologic problems.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Solute carrier family 13 member 1 ([Q9BZW2](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9BZW2/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Sodium:sulfate symporter that mediates sulfate reabsorption in the kidney and small intestine. Can also mediate the transport of selenate and thiosulfate (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Apical cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Highly expressed in kidney; not detectable in the other tissues tested.
