# BHMT: betaine--homocysteine S-methyltransferase

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 5 (methylation and one-carbon metabolism). Encodes a cytosolic enzyme that catalyzes the conversion of betaine and homocysteine to dimethylglycine and methionine, respectively. Defects in this gene could lead to hyperhomocyst(e)inemia, but such a defect has not yet been observed.

**Corpus relevance:** One-carbon metabolism supplies methyl groups for DNA/histone methylation, the epigenetic layer implicated in persistent post-drug changes.

**Protein (UniProt):** Betaine--homocysteine S-methyltransferase 1 ([Q93088](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q93088/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Involved in the regulation of homocysteine metabolism. Converts betaine and homocysteine to dimethylglycine and methionine, respectively. This reaction is also required for the irreversible oxidation of choline.

**Pathway (UniProt):** Amine and polyamine degradation; betaine degradation; sarcosine from betaine: step 1/2. Amino-acid biosynthesis; L-methionine biosynthesis via de novo pathway; L-methionine from L-homocysteine (BhmT route): step 1/1.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm, cytosol; Nucleus.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Found exclusively in liver and kidney.
