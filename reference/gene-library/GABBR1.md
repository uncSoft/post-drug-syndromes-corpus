# GABBR1: gamma-aminobutyric acid type B receptor subunit 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 6 (neurotransmission). Encodes a receptor for gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), which is the main inhibitory neurotransmitter in the mammalian central nervous system. This receptor functions as a heterodimer with GABA(B) receptor 2.

**Corpus relevance:** Monoamine, GABA, glutamate, and cholinergic systems are the direct pharmacologic targets of SSRIs/SNRIs (PSSD) and the downstream effectors of neurosteroid signaling (PFS).

**Protein (UniProt):** Gamma-aminobutyric acid type B receptor subunit 1 ([Q9UBS5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9UBS5/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Component of a heterodimeric G protein-coupled receptor for GABA, formed by GABBR1 and GABBR2. Within the heterodimeric GABA receptor, only GABBR1 seems to bind agonists, while GABBR2 mediates coupling to G proteins. Ligand binding causes a conformation change that triggers signaling via guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) and modulates the activity of down-stream effectors, such as adenylate cyclase. Signaling inhibits adenylate cyclase, stimulates phospholipase A2, activates potassium channels, inactivates voltage-dependent calcium-channels and modulates inositol phospholipid hydrolysis. Calcium is required for high affinity binding to GABA (By similarity). Plays a critical role in the fine-tuning of inhibitory synaptic transmission.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cell membrane; Postsynaptic cell membrane; Cell projection, dendrite; Secreted.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Highly expressed in brain. Weakly expressed in heart, small intestine and uterus. Isoform 1A: Mainly expressed in granular cell and molecular layer. Isoform 1B: Mainly expressed in Purkinje cells. Isoform 1E: Predominantly expressed in peripheral tissues as kidney, lung, trachea, colon, small intestine, stomach, bone marrow, thymus and mammary gland.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Neurodevelopmental disorder with language delay and variable cognitive abnormalities (NEDLC).
