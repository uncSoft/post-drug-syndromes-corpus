# LRP2: LDL receptor related protein 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 2 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). The protein encoded by this gene, low density lipoprotein-related protein 2 (LRP2) or megalin, is a multi-ligand endocytic receptor that is expressed in many different tissues but primarily in absorptive epithilial tissues such as the kidney. This glycoprotein has a large amino-terminal extracellular domain, a single transmembrane domain, and a short carboxy-terminal cytoplasmic tail.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Powers note (his unpublished theorizing, research only, not medical advice):** Named by Powers (Reddit, 16 Oct 2026; PRH-1550), who says a large share of PFS patients carry major mutations in LRP2, though not all; no study in the library reports this.

**Protein (UniProt):** Low-density lipoprotein receptor-related protein 2 ([P98164](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P98164/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Multiligand endocytic receptor (By similarity). Acts together with CUBN to mediate endocytosis of high-density lipoproteins (By similarity). Mediates receptor-mediated uptake of polybasic drugs such as aprotinin, aminoglycosides and polymyxin B (By similarity). In the kidney, mediates the tubular uptake and clearance of leptin (By similarity). Also mediates transport of leptin across the blood-brain barrier through endocytosis at the choroid plexus epithelium (By similarity). Endocytosis of leptin in neuronal cells is required for hypothalamic leptin signaling and leptin-mediated regulation of feeding and body weight (By similarity). Mediates endocytosis and subsequent lysosomal degradation of CST3 in kidney proximal tubule cells (By similarity). Mediates renal uptake of 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 in complex with the vitamin D3 transporter GC/DBP (By similarity). Mediates renal uptake of metallothionein-bound heavy metals.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Apical cell membrane; Endosome lumen; Membrane, coated pit; Cell projection, dendrite; Cell projection, axon.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in first and third trimester cytotrophoblasts in the placenta (at protein level). Absorptive epithelia, including renal proximal tubules.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Donnai-Barrow syndrome (DBS).
