# HNF4A: hepatocyte nuclear factor 4 alpha

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 20 (steroid and nuclear hormone receptors, cell signaling and transcription factors). The protein encoded by this gene is a nuclear transcription factor which binds DNA as a homodimer. The protein controls the expression of several genes, including hepatocyte nuclear factor 1 alpha, a transcription factor which regulates the expression of several hepatic genes.

**Corpus relevance:** Nuclear receptors transduce steroid signals into lasting gene-expression programs; persistent receptor-level remodeling (or silencing) is a leading hypothesis for symptoms that outlast drug exposure. General signaling/transcription machinery; no direct post-drug-syndrome link established for most members; included for completeness of the panel.

**Protein (UniProt):** Hepatocyte nuclear factor 4-alpha ([P41235](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P41235/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Transcriptional regulator which controls the expression of hepatic genes during the transition of endodermal cells to hepatic progenitor cells, facilitating the recruitment of RNA pol II to the promoters of target genes. Activates the transcription of CYP2C38 (By similarity). Represses the CLOCK-BMAL1 transcriptional activity and is essential for circadian rhythm maintenance and period regulation in the liver and colon cells.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Maturity-onset diabetes of the young 1 (MODY1); Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2D); Fanconi renotubular syndrome 4 with maturity-onset diabetes of the young (FRTS4).
