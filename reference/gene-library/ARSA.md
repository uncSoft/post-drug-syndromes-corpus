# ARSA: arylsulfatase A

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 22 (steroid conjugation and clearance). The protein encoded by this gene hydrolyzes cerebroside sulfate to cerebroside and sulfate. Defects in this gene lead to metachromatic leucodystrophy (MLD), a progressive demyelination disease which results in a variety of neurological symptoms and ultimately death.

**Corpus relevance:** Steroid clearance is the exit route for androgens and their metabolites, the pathway at the center of Powers' PFS model (defective glucuronidation/sulfation trapping androgens intracellularly). Gut bacterial enzymes can undo this clearance, linking it to the microbiome thread.

**Protein (UniProt):** Arylsulfatase A ([P15289](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P15289/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Lysosomal enzyme that catalyzes the hydrolysis of cerebroside-3-sulfate (N-acyl-1-beta-D-(3-O-sulfo)-galactosyl-sphing-4-enine) into cerebroside and sulfate, a reaction that requires the activator protein saposin B. Also involved in the catabolism of other sulfoglycosphingolipids such as SB1a and SM3 (beta-D-3-sulfogalactosyl-(1->4)-beta-D-glucosyl-(1<->1')-N-acylsphing-4-enine) (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Endoplasmic reticulum; Lysosome.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Metachromatic leukodystrophy (MLD); Multiple sulfatase deficiency (MSD).
