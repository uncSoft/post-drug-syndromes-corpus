# KDM5A: lysine demethylase 5A

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 12 (epigenetic regulation). Encodes a member of the Jumonji, AT-rich interactive domain 1 (JARID1) histone demethylase protein family. The protein plays a role in gene regulation through the histone code by specifically demethylating lysine 4 of histone H3.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Lysine-specific demethylase 5A ([P29375](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P29375/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Histone demethylase that specifically demethylates 'Lys-4' of histone H3, thereby playing a central role in histone code. Does not demethylate histone H3 'Lys-9', H3 'Lys-27', H3 'Lys-36', H3 'Lys-79' or H4 'Lys-20'. Demethylates trimethylated and dimethylated but not monomethylated H3 'Lys-4'. Regulates specific gene transcription through DNA-binding on 5'-CCGCCC-3' motif. May stimulate transcription mediated by nuclear receptors. Involved in transcriptional regulation of Hox proteins during cell differentiation. May participate in transcriptional repression of cytokines such as CXCL12. Plays a role in the regulation of the circadian rhythm and in maintaining the normal periodicity of the circadian clock. In a histone demethylase-independent manner, acts as a coactivator of the CLOCK-BMAL1-mediated transcriptional activation of PER1/2 and other clock-controlled genes and increases histone acetylation at PER1/2 promoters by inhibiting the activity of HDAC1 (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus, nucleolus; Nucleus.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** El Hayek-Chahrour neurodevelopmental syndrome (NEDEHC).
