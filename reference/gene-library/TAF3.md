# TAF3: TATA-box binding protein associated factor 3

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 10 (basal transcription machinery). The highly conserved RNA polymerase II transcription factor TFIID (see TAF1; MIM 313650) comprises the TATA box-binding protein (TBP; MIM 600075) and a set of TBP-associated factors (TAFs), including TAF3. TAFs contribute to promoter recognition and selectivity and act as antiapoptotic factors (Gangloff et al., 2001 ).

**Corpus relevance:** Core RNA polymerase II machinery; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Transcription initiation factor TFIID subunit 3 ([Q5VWG9](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q5VWG9/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** The TFIID basal transcription factor complex plays a major role in the initiation of RNA polymerase II (Pol II)-dependent transcription. TFIID recognizes and binds promoters with or without a TATA box via its subunit TBP, a TATA-box-binding protein, and promotes assembly of the pre-initiation complex (PIC). The TFIID complex consists of TBP and TBP-associated factors (TAFs), including TAF1, TAF2, TAF3, TAF4, TAF5, TAF6, TAF7, TAF8, TAF9, TAF10, TAF11, TAF12 and TAF13. The TFIID complex structure can be divided into 3 modules TFIID-A, TFIID-B, and TFIID-C. TAF3 forms the TFIID-A module together with TAF5 and TBP. Required in complex with TBPL2 for the differentiation of myoblasts into myocytes. The TAF3-TBPL2 complex replaces TFIID at specific promoters at an early stage in the differentiation process.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus.
