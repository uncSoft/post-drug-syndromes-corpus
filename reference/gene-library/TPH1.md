# TPH1: tryptophan hydroxylase 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (neurotransmission). Encodes a member of the aromatic amino acid hydroxylase family. The protein catalyzes the first and rate limiting step in the biosynthesis of serotonin, an important hormone and neurotransmitter.

**Corpus relevance:** Monoamine, GABA, glutamate, and cholinergic systems are the direct pharmacologic targets of SSRIs/SNRIs (PSSD) and the downstream effectors of neurosteroid signaling (PFS).

**Protein (UniProt):** Tryptophan 5-hydroxylase 1 ([P17752](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P17752/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Oxidizes L-tryptophan to 5-hydroxy-l-tryptophan in the rate-determining step of serotonin biosynthesis (By similarity).

**Pathway (UniProt):** Aromatic compound metabolism; serotonin biosynthesis; serotonin from L-tryptophan: step 1/2 (By similarity).

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Isoform 2: Seems to be less widely expressed than isoform 1.
