# WAPL: WAPL cohesin release factor

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 10 (chromosome cohesion and architecture). Enables ATP-dependent protein-DNA unloader activity. Involved in several processes, including mitotic sister chromatid segregation; negative regulation of DNA replication; and negative regulation of sister chromatid cohesion.

**Corpus relevance:** Sister-chromatid cohesion and genome architecture; general cellular function with no direct post-drug-syndrome link established.

**Protein (UniProt):** Wings apart-like protein homolog ([Q7Z5K2](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q7Z5K2/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Regulator of sister chromatid cohesion in mitosis which negatively regulates cohesin association with chromatin. Involved in both sister chromatid cohesion during interphase and sister-chromatid resolution during early stages of mitosis. Couples DNA replication to sister chromatid cohesion. Cohesion ensures that chromosome partitioning is accurate in both meiotic and mitotic cells and plays an important role in DNA repair.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Chromosome; Cytoplasm.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Isoform 1 is highly expressed in uterine cervix tumor. Isoform 2 is widely expressed with a high level in skeletal muscle and heart.
