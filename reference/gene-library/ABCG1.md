# ABCG1: ATP binding cassette subfamily G member 1

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 21 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The protein encoded by this gene is a member of the superfamily of ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters. ABC proteins transport various molecules across extra- and intra-cellular membranes.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** ATP-binding cassette sub-family G member 1 ([P45844](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P45844/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Catalyzes the efflux of phospholipids such as sphingomyelin, cholesterol and its oxygenated derivatives like 7beta-hydroxycholesterol and this transport is coupled to hydrolysis of ATP. The lipid efflux is ALB-dependent. Is an active component of the macrophage lipid export complex. Could also be involved in intracellular lipid transport processes. The role in cellular lipid homeostasis may not be limited to macrophages. Prevents cell death by transporting cytotoxic 7beta-hydroxycholesterol.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Endoplasmic reticulum membrane; Golgi apparatus membrane; Cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Expressed in several tissues. Expressed in macrophages; expression is increased in macrophages from patients with Tangier disease.
