# INO80B: INO80 complex subunit B

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 2 (epigenetic regulation). Encodes a subunit of an ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling complex, INO80, which plays a role in DNA and nucleosome-activated ATPase activity and ATP-dependent nucleosome sliding. Readthrough transcription of this gene into the neighboring downstream gene, which encodes WW domain-binding protein 1, generates a non-coding transcript.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** INO80 complex subunit B ([Q9C086](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q9C086/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Induces growth and cell cycle arrests at the G1 phase of the cell cycle. Proposed core component of the chromatin remodeling INO80 complex which is involved in transcriptional regulation, DNA replication and probably DNA repair.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus; Nucleus, nucleolus.
