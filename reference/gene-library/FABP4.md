# FABP4: fatty acid binding protein 4

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 8 (lipid and cholesterol homeostasis). FABP4 encodes the fatty acid binding protein found in adipocytes. Fatty acid binding proteins are a family of small, highly conserved, cytoplasmic proteins that bind long-chain fatty acids and other hydrophobic ligands.

**Corpus relevance:** Cholesterol is the obligate precursor of all steroid hormones; lipid handling sets substrate supply for steroidogenesis and neurosteroid synthesis.

**Protein (UniProt):** Fatty acid-binding protein, adipocyte ([P15090](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P15090/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Lipid transport protein in adipocytes. Binds both long chain fatty acids and retinoic acid. Delivers long-chain fatty acids and retinoic acid to their cognate receptors in the nucleus (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Cytoplasm; Nucleus.
