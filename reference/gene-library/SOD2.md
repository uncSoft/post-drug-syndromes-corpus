# SOD2: superoxide dismutase 2

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 6 (mitochondrial function and energy). This gene is a member of the iron/manganese superoxide dismutase family. It encodes a mitochondrial protein that forms a homotetramer and binds one manganese ion per subunit.

**Corpus relevance:** No record in the library measures mitochondrial function or oxidative stress in people with these syndromes; the records that touch either are animal or cell work, and they point in mixed directions. These genes are here for their place in energy metabolism, not for an established link.

**Protein (UniProt):** Superoxide dismutase [Mn], mitochondrial ([P04179](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04179/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Destroys superoxide anion radicals which are normally produced within the cells and which are toxic to biological systems.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Mitochondrion matrix.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Microvascular complications of diabetes 6 (MVCD6).
