# CTCF: CCCTC-binding factor

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 16 (epigenetic regulation). This gene is a member of the BORIS + CTCF gene family and encodes a transcriptional regulator protein with 11 highly conserved zinc finger (ZF) domains. This nuclear protein is able to use different combinations of the ZF domains to bind different DNA target sequences and proteins.

**Corpus relevance:** Epigenetic persistence is one proposed explanation for why post-drug syndromes endure after the drug is gone, and no study in the library shows an epigenetic change outlasting the drug that caused it; chromatin regulators are candidates for a mechanism that is still unproven.

**Protein (UniProt):** Transcriptional repressor CTCF ([P49711](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P49711/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt, excerpt):** Chromatin binding factor that binds to DNA sequence specific sites and regulates the 3D structure of chromatin. Binds together strands of DNA, thus forming chromatin loops, and anchors DNA to cellular structures, such as the nuclear lamina. Defines the boundaries between active and heterochromatic DNA via binding to chromatin insulators, thereby preventing interaction between promoter and nearby enhancers and silencers. Participates in the allele-specific gene expression at the imprinted IGF2/H19 gene locus. On the maternal allele, binding within the H19 imprinting control region (ICR) mediates maternally inherited higher-order chromatin conformation to restrict enhancer access to IGF2 (By similarity). Mediates interchromosomal association between IGF2/H19 and WSB1/NF1 and may direct distant DNA segments to a common transcription factory (By similarity). Regulates asynchronous replication of IGF2/H19 (By similarity).

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Nucleus, nucleoplasm; Chromosome; Chromosome, centromere.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Ubiquitous. Absent in primary spermatocytes.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Intellectual developmental disorder, autosomal dominant 21 (MRD21).
