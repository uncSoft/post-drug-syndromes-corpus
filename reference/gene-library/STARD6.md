# STARD6: StAR related lipid transfer domain containing 6

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 18 (steroid hormone synthesis). Cholesterol homeostasis is regulated, at least in part, by sterol regulatory element (SRE)-binding proteins (e.g., SREBP1; MIM 184756) and by liver X receptors (e.g., LXRA; MIM 602423). Upon sterol depletion, LXRs are inactive and SREBPs are cleaved, after which they bind promoter SREs and activate genes involved in cholesterol biosynthesis and uptake.

**Corpus relevance:** Steroid-synthesis enzymes sit directly on the pathways perturbed by 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors and SSRIs; inherited variation here is a candidate susceptibility factor for persistent post-drug phenotypes.

**Protein (UniProt):** StAR-related lipid transfer protein 6 ([P59095](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P59095/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** May be involved in the intracellular transport of sterols or other lipids. May bind cholesterol or other sterols (By similarity).
