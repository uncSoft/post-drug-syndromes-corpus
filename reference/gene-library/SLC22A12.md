# SLC22A12: solute carrier family 22 member 12

A protein-coding gene on chromosome 11 (drug and xenobiotic transporters). The protein encoded by this gene is a member of the organic anion transporter (OAT) family, and it acts as a urate transporter to regulate urate levels in blood. This protein is an integral membrane protein primarily found in epithelial cells of the proximal tubule of the kidney.

**Corpus relevance:** Transporters set systemic exposure to finasteride/SSRIs and clear conjugated steroid metabolites, directly relevant to both drug pharmacokinetics and Powers' clearance-centered PFS mechanism.

**Protein (UniProt):** Solute carrier family 22 member 12 ([Q96S37](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q96S37/entry), reviewed)

**Function (UniProt):** Electroneutral antiporter that translocates urate across the apical membrane of proximal tubular cells in exchange for monovalent organic or inorganic anions. Involved in renal reabsorption of urate and helps maintaining blood levels of uric acid. Mediates urate uptake by an exchange with organic anions such as (S)-lactate and nicotinate, and inorganic anion Cl(-). Other inorganic anions such as Br(-), I(-) and NO3(-) may also act as counteranions that exchange for urate. Also mediates orotate tubular uptake coupled with nicotinate efflux and to a lesser extent with lactate efflux, therefore displaying a potential role in orotate renal reabsorption. Orotate transport is Cl(-)-dependent.

**Subcellular location (UniProt):** Apical cell membrane.

**Tissue specificity (UniProt):** Detected in kidney (at protein level). Detected in fetal and adult kidney. Detected in epithelial cells of proximal tubules in renal cortex.

**Associated conditions (UniProt):** Hypouricemia renal 1 (RHUC1).
